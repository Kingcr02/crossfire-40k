* = accused of using Ricky Dice, one per game. Pts = record points. Quality = quality of wins. Margin = capped margin (each game capped at ±20). Full details below.

How rankings work

Applied in this order. Each step is only used if players are still tied.
  1. Record. Win = 3 points, draw = 1, loss = 0. Most points ranks highest. A win is worth three draws, so playing for the win pays off.
  2. Head-to-head. If exactly two players are tied and they played each other, the winner of that game ranks higher.
  3. Quality of wins. Add up the final record points of every opponent you beat, plus half for any opponent you drew. Beating stronger opponents counts for more. No VP or margins are used in this step.
  4. Capped margin. Your VP margin in each game, capped at ±20, added together. A close win still beats a loss, but a 60-point blowout counts the same as a 20-point win. This keeps armies that naturally run up the score from winning ties on that alone.
  5. Result vs. strongest opponent. Your result against the highest-placed opponent you faced. Win beats draw beats loss. Same result: the capped margin of that one game decides it.
  6. Wins going second. Wins where your opponent took the first turn. Needs "went first" ticked when results are entered.
  7. Shared place. Still level after all of that: the players share the place.
